01 / Deploying

Keep a service in view.

Follow new log lines while you deploy. Watch the volume and severity of arriving records, then freeze the display to review recent errors.

tell-tail service.log --window 10
02 / Debugging

Watch a worker’s output.

Pipe stdout and stderr straight into a dashboard. Unbuffered output helps records arrive as the process writes them.

python3 -u worker.py 2>&1 | tell-tail

Small, local, inspectable

Keep the view
close to the source.

Tell-Tail keeps counters, rolling statistics, and a bounded sample of recent errors in memory. Statistics reset on restart. It works offline, and webhook notifications are optional.

Log ratios describe the records received. They do not establish application health. For persistent watches, retained evidence, and durable notification retries, explore Ding.
